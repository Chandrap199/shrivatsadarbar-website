Return & Refund Policy

We are committed to providing premium quality Laddu Gopal products and a transparent return process.

Effective Date: 14 July 2026

At ShriVatsaDarbar, every order is carefully checked before dispatch. If you receive a damaged, incorrect or incomplete order, we will be happy to assist you according to the policy below.

1. Return Window

Customers may request a return, replacement or refund within 3 days from the date of delivery. Requests received after this period may not be accepted.

2. Eligible Reasons

Returns and refunds are accepted only in the following cases:

  • Damaged product received.
  • Wrong product delivered.
  • Manufacturing defect.
  • Missing item(s) in the parcel.

3. Mandatory Continuous Unboxing Video

To ensure a fair process for both customers and ShriVatsaDarbar, a continuous unboxing video is mandatory for all damage, missing item, wrong product and refund claims.

  • Start recording before opening the sealed parcel.
  • Clearly show the shipping label.
  • Open the parcel in one continuous video without any cuts.
  • Clearly show the product and any issue.
  • Share the complete video on our WhatsApp number for verification.

Claims submitted without a continuous unboxing video may not be eligible for return, replacement or refund.

4. Product Return Conditions

To qualify for a return, the product must:

  • Be unused and in its original condition.
  • Be returned with the original packaging.
  • Include all original tags and labels.
  • Include all accessories received with the order.
  • Be packed safely to avoid damage during return transit.

5. Return Approval Process

After receiving your return request, our team will review the unboxing video and order details. If the request is found to be genuine, we will approve the return.

Once approved, customers may choose one of the following options:

  • Replacement (subject to product availability).
  • Refund.

6. Refund Process

After your return request is approved, our support team will guide you through the return process.

The returned product must reach ShriVatsaDarbar in its original condition with:

  • Original packaging.
  • All tags and labels attached.
  • Any accessories delivered with the product.

After we receive the returned product and it successfully passes our quality inspection, your refund will be processed to the original payment method or any mutually agreed payment method.

If you choose a replacement instead of a refund, the replacement product will be dispatched after the returned product has been received and approved, subject to stock availability.

7. Non-Returnable Cases

  • Return request raised after 3 days from the date of delivery.
  • Product has been used, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
  • Original packaging, tags, labels or accessories are missing.
  • No continuous unboxing video is provided.
  • The returned product does not match the product originally delivered.
  • Damage caused due to improper handling or misuse after delivery.
