At ShriVatsaDarbar, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you visit our website or contact us through WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name

  • Shipping Address
  • Order Details

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

Currently, ShriVatsaDarbar accepts orders through WhatsApp. Payments are collected directly through approved payment methods shared during order confirmation. We do not store your banking or card information on our website.

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and understand website traffic. You can disable cookies anytime through your browser settings.

5. Information Sharing

We never sell or rent your personal information. Your information is shared only when necessary to complete deliveries or comply with legal requirements.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable security practices to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or misuse.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us anytime to:

  • Access your information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy whenever required. Any changes will be published on this page.
