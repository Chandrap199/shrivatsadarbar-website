Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Laddu Gopal Poshak, orders, shipping, payments, returns and customer support.

Home › FAQ
How do I place an order?

Browse your favorite products, select the appropriate size, and place your order through our website or contact us on WhatsApp for quick assistance.

What sizes are available for Laddu Gopal Poshak?

We offer multiple sizes suitable for Laddu Gopal idols. Each product page includes available sizes to help you choose the perfect fit.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We deliver across India using trusted courier partners to ensure your order reaches you safely.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days. Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept secure online payments through our website. Available payment options may include UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking and other supported payment methods during checkout.

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?

Cash on Delivery availability depends on your delivery location. If COD is available for your PIN code, it will be shown during checkout.

Can I exchange or return a product?

Please refer to our Return & Refund Policy. If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, contact us within the specified time along with photos for quick assistance.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders can usually be cancelled before they are shipped. Once dispatched, cancellation may not be possible. Please contact us immediately if you need to cancel an order.

Do you offer custom-made Poshak?

Yes. For selected designs and festive collections, custom orders may be available. Please contact us on WhatsApp to discuss your requirements.

How can I contact ShriVatsaDarbar?

You can reach us through WhatsApp, email, or our Contact Us page. We are happy to help with product selection, sizing and order-related queries.

How should I care for Laddu Gopal Poshak?

Handle the fabric gently and keep it clean and dry. Delicate hand washing is recommended unless specific care instructions are provided with the product.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is always ready to help you choose the perfect Laddu Gopal Poshak, Mukut and divine accessories. Contact us anytime and we'll be happy to assist you.
