Cancellation Policy

Please read our cancellation policy carefully before placing your order.

Effective Date: 14 July 2026

At ShriVatsaDarbar, we strive to process and dispatch every order as quickly as possible. Please review our cancellation policy below.

1. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of their order only before the order has been dispatched.

Once the order has been shipped, cancellation requests cannot be accepted.

2. How to Request Cancellation

To request cancellation, please contact us as soon as possible with your order details through:

  • WhatsApp: +91 88261 96544
  • Email: shrivatsadarbar@gmail.com

3. Cancellation Approval

Our team will verify the order status. If the order has not yet been dispatched, your cancellation request will be approved.

If the order has already been handed over to the courier partner, cancellation will no longer be possible.

4. Customized Products

Orders for customized, personalized or made-to-order products cannot be cancelled once production or customization has started.

5. Refund for Cancelled Orders

If your cancellation request is approved before dispatch, any payment received by ShriVatsaDarbar will be refunded to the original payment method or another mutually agreed payment method.

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–7 business days. The actual credit may depend on your bank or payment provider.

6. Refusal of Delivery

If a customer refuses to accept a parcel after it has been shipped, the order will not be treated as a cancelled order.

Any refund, if applicable, will be processed only after the parcel is returned to ShriVatsaDarbar. Shipping charges and any return shipping costs may be deducted from the refund amount.

7. Changes to Orders

If you wish to modify your shipping address, contact number or other order details, please contact us immediately. We will make every effort to accommodate your request before the order is dispatched.
