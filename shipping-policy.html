Shipping Policy

Learn about our order processing, shipping timelines, delivery methods, and important information regarding shipments from ShriVatsaDarbar.

Home › Shipping Policy

Effective Date: 14 July 2026

Thank you for shopping with ShriVatsaDarbar. We are committed to delivering every Laddu Gopal Poshak and divine accessory safely, securely and on time.

1. Shipping Coverage

We currently deliver across India. If you require international shipping, please contact us on WhatsApp before placing your order.

2. Order Confirmation

All orders are confirmed through WhatsApp. Before dispatch, we verify the product, size, quantity, delivery address and payment details to ensure a smooth shopping experience.

3. Order Processing Time

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days after order confirmation. During festivals, holidays or high-demand periods, processing may take a little longer.

4. Estimated Delivery Time

  • North India – 2 to 5 Business Days
  • West India – 3 to 6 Business Days
  • South India – 4 to 7 Business Days
  • North-East & Remote Locations – 5 to 9 Business Days

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary depending on the courier partner and local conditions.

5. Shipping Charges

🚚 FREE Shipping on all orders of ₹999 and above.

Orders below ₹999 will attract a flat shipping charge of ₹80.

Any applicable shipping charges will always be communicated and confirmed during your WhatsApp order confirmation before payment is collected.

6. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, the courier tracking details will be shared with you through WhatsApp whenever available.

7. Delayed Deliveries

While we strive to deliver every order on time, delays caused by weather conditions, public holidays, festivals, courier service issues or unforeseen circumstances may occasionally occur.

8. Incorrect Shipping Address

Customers are requested to provide complete and accurate delivery information. ShriVatsaDarbar shall not be responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect shipping details.

9. Damaged or Missing Items

If your parcel arrives damaged or if any item is missing, please contact us within 24 hours of receiving the package and share clear photographs of the parcel and product for prompt assistance.
